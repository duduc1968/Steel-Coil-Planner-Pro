# Steel Coil Planner Pro v9.3.6 — Intelligent Hold Selection

## Implemented
- Selecting a hold with existing cargo zones opens its most recently edited zone.
- Selecting an empty hold immediately displays **Create New Zone** options.
- The blank “No cargo zone selected” workflow is removed from Cargo Zones.
- New-zone options include planning mode, required weight, initial fixed length,
  tolerance, cargo type, name, and operational notes.
- No zone is stored until **Create New Zone** is pressed.
- Required Cargo Weight mode leaves the final longitudinal length to Optimize Preview.
- Hold 1 remains forward and displayed at the right-hand end of the cargo plan.

## Engine protection
The validated width arrangement, geometry, optimizer, cargo reservation, and
progressive validation engines are unchanged.
