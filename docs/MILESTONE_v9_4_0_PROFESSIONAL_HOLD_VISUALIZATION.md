# v9.4.0 — Professional Hold Visualization

- PORT and STARBOARD labels are outside the hold boundary in Cargo Zones and Stowage Workspace.
- The hold border now represents only the true cargo-hold width.
- Coil rows use the complete internal width of the hold drawing.
- Cross Section has visible port/starboard boundaries and tank top.
- Cargo-zone cards have more room for long holds.
- Grid Auto mode uses 1 m up to 20 m and 2 m above 20 m.
- Manual 1 m and 2 m grid selections remain available.

Validated cargo geometry and planning logic were not changed.
