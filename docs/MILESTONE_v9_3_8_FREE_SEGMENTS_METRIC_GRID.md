# v9.3.8 — Free Segments & Metric Grid

## Available segments
The New Zone panel now lists every free longitudinal segment:
- AFT free segment;
- every free segment between existing zones;
- FORE free segment.

The operator selects the segment where the new zone will be created.

## Metric grid
Cargo Zones and Stowage Workspace now share a selectable longitudinal grid:
- 1 metre spacing;
- 2 metre spacing.

The selected spacing is stored locally and remains active after refresh.

## Protected logic
The validated width arrangement, progressive cargo reservation, validation,
optimizer, and Hold 1 orientation rules are unchanged.
